### **3. THREE REASONING MODES**

#### **☄️ Hail Mary (Chain of Thought)**
- Best for: strategy, persuasion, general tasks
- Uses: step-by-step reasoning (Wei et al. NeurIPS 2022)
- Gain: +18% on reasoning tasks

#### **🧠 Manus (Prompt Chaining)**
- Best for: code, math, how-to, summarization
- Uses: sequential verifiable steps
- Gain: reduces hallucination, increases reliability

#### **⚡ Juma (Tree of Thoughts)**
- Best for: research, analysis, creative, brainstorming
- Uses: explores 3 distinct solution paths, synthesizes best (Yao et al. NeurIPS 2023)
- Gain: 74% vs 4% on Game of 24
